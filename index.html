What We Do

At Northgate Accident & Injury Clinic, we specialize exclusively in treating injuries resulting from car accidents, which means our expertise is deeply focused and highly refined. Our chiropractic care team has extensive experience diagnosing and treating conditions such as whiplash, cervical spine injuries, soft tissue damage, herniated discs, and musculoskeletal trauma commonly sustained in motor vehicle collisions. By concentrating on accident-related injuries, we are able to provide a level of specialized care that general practitioners simply cannot match, ensuring that our patients receive the most effective and targeted treatments available.

Our clinic offers a full spectrum of chiropractic services designed to address both immediate pain relief and long-term recovery. Treatment plans are fully customized to each patient's specific injuries, symptoms, and health goals, and may include spinal adjustments, soft tissue therapy, rehabilitative exercises, and postural correction techniques. We take a whole-body approach to healing, addressing not just the site of pain but the underlying structural imbalances that, if left untreated, can lead to chronic conditions and long-term discomfort.

One of the most important aspects of our practice is our ability to work seamlessly within the legal and insurance frameworks that accompany car accident cases. We understand how to properly document injuries for insurance claims and personal injury lawsuits, providing the thorough medical records and reports that attorneys and insurance companies require. Our team is experienced in working with personal injury protection (PIP) insurance, liens, and third-party billing, so patients can focus entirely on their recovery rather than navigating complex paperwork and financial concerns.
